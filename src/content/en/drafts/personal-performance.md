# Why I Care About Energy, Not Just Productivity

For a long time, I focused only on tools and workflows.

But performance is not just about that.

---

## The missing piece

Energy.

- sleep
- focus
- mental clarity

---

## What I noticed

On low-energy days:
- tools don’t matter
- productivity drops

---

## What I changed

- better sleep
- fewer distractions
- more intentional breaks

---

## Result

Better output with less effort.

---

Tools help.  
Energy decides how well you use them.
